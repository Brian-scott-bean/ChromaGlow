Support

ChromaGlow is currently in beta. If something isn't working the way you expect, here's what to try and how to reach me.

Common issues

Bridge won't connect

Make sure your phone and Hue Bridge are on the same Wi-Fi network. Tap the bridge when it appears in the discovery screen, then press the physical button on top of the bridge when prompted.

Lights don't respond in Sync mode

Open the Sync tab, select a room, and tap Start. Make sure the mic sensitivity slider isn't all the way to the left. Try Gaming mode first — it's the most responsive.

Scenes not showing up

Pull down to refresh on the Scenes tab. If scenes were created in another app they should appear after a refresh.

Scheduled automation didn't fire

Make sure you've allowed notification permissions for ChromaGlow in iOS Settings. The app uses notifications to trigger automations — without permission they won't run.

Get in touch

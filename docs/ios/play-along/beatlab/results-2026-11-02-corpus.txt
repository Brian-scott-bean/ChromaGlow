Beat lab on real recordings — build 914 baseline (2026-11-02)
==============================================================

What ships today (OnsetFrameAnalyzer + BeatTracker + BeatClock, `runProd`), on the licence-clean corpus at
/Volumes/Dev/Datasets/chromaglow-beat-corpus (audio never in this repo). Reproduce:

  docs/ios/play-along/beatlab/run.sh -import
  docs/ios/play-along/beatlab/run.sh -corpus -set <set> -v
  docs/ios/play-along/beatlab/run.sh -drums

Corpus (all allow commercial use; raw/<set>/SOURCE.txt):
  gmd        Groove MIDI Dataset — 347 drum performances in 4/4 ("beat" takes with audio), beats from the click
  slakh      BabySlakh — 20 full multi-instrument mixes rendered from MIDI (bass, keys, drums…)
  guitarset  GuitarSet — 360 solo acoustic guitar excerpts (click grid; no drums)
  candombe   Candombe — 35 Afro-Uruguayan drum recordings, beats and downbeats marked by hand
  rooms      BUT ReverbDB — 2,325 measured room responses: 527 home-like (C50 ≥ 5 dB, RT60 ≤ 0.8 s) in 5 rooms
             → 40 used; 1,798 halls / far from the speaker in 9 rooms → 27 used. Recorded at 16 kHz: the direct
             sound's top octave is added back (RoomSim.withAir) rather than losing every hi-hat.
Conditions: line (the file), room (home-like room + phone mic band + noise −30 dB), speaker (small speaker in a
home-like room, noise −24 dB), hall (long or dominant echo).

Scores: identical to mir_eval 0.8.2 (`-metrics` cross-check, 200 random cases incl. double/half/off-beat: worst
difference 4.9e-7). "beat F" = the beats the LIGHTS would play — the clock's grid between tracker passes, only while
Play Along trusts it (phase confidence ≥ 0.5). "grid F" = the grid whenever the clock holds a tempo, trusted or not.

Reading it:
  • The lights play along on very little real music. Clean BabySlakh mixes: F 0.56 (7 of 20 never lock); clean
    drum performances: 0.18 (237 of 347 never lock); solo guitar: 0.06.
  • The tracker usually HEARS the right tempo — it never trusts it. Traced on Groove MIDI "funk-groove1 138": the
    tempo is 138–139 BPM within 6 s, the tempo confidence 0.00 for the whole take, so the clock never adopts it.
    The confidence reads the onset autocorrelation at the EXACT beat lag; human timing spreads one-frame (10 ms)
    onset spikes a frame or two apart, and the threshold was calibrated on the lab's machine-timed synthetic songs.
    Fix (build 915): smooth the onset signal before the tempo and phase combs — see results-2026-11-02-tracker.txt.
  • A room costs about a third of what's left (Slakh 0.56 → 0.38); a hall nearly all of it.

Synthetic rooms (exponential noise tails, flat spectrum) were far kinder: RT60 0.4 s +6 dB → 0.89, 0.8 s 0 dB →
0.88, 1.2 s −4 dB → 0.68. Measured rooms ring longer in the bass, where the kick lives. A phone across a living room
(C50 ~5–15 dB) is the "room" condition; a lecture hall or the far end of a big room is "hall".

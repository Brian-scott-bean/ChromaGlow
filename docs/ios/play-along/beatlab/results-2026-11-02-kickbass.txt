Kick vs bass — build 915 (2026-11-02). run.sh -kickbass: the -drumtune set (24 Groove MIDI takes + 20 BabySlakh
mixes, line + a measured home-like room), ±50 ms; recall on strokes ≥ velocity 40, precision against every stroke
of the drum's family. Each kick band is one 47 Hz FFT bin, so a bass note that starts from silence rises like a
kick; BabySlakh (full mixes with bass) is where that shows, as kick precision.

Ways tried, starting from the lanes as handed over (pass 2):
  level gate N      a kick must bring the kick bands within N (log units, summed over 3 bins) of the loudest low
                    end lately (fading with a 4 s time constant). No wait.
  confirm F decay D a kick is published only once the low end falls by D within F frames (a kick dies away, a bass
                    note holds) — the light is F × 10 ms late.
  snare veto W      no snare on a frame the kick lane fired on, nor within W after one.
  hold 1            a snare is held one frame and dropped if the kick lane fires in it.

Decision: level gate 2 + snare veto 50 ms + hold 1, no confirmation ("standard (build 915)", last row). It reaches
the precision 120 ms of confirmation reaches on mixes (kick P 0.62 → 0.70) with no wait, and the veto + hold lift
snare precision on mixes 0.45 → 0.67 (recall 0.72 → 0.55: fewer false flashes, some missed snares). The hold costs
snares 10 ms. Known limit, kept as an expected failure in DrumLanesTests: a bass line in a song with NO drums still
reads as kicks (its notes are the loudest low end there is), and about a second into a drum break with the bass
going on, bass notes pass the gate again.

Beat lab on real recordings — build 915 tracker (2026-11-02)
============================================================

The shipping chain (OnsetFrameAnalyzer + BeatTracker + BeatClock, `runProd`) with build 915's tracker
(BeatTrackerTuning.standard: Hann smoothing over 9 frames, confidence floor 0.16), on the same corpus and
conditions as the 914 baseline (results-2026-11-02-corpus.txt). Reproduce:

  docs/ios/play-along/beatlab/run.sh -corpus -set <set> -v      (one process per set)

"beat F" = the beats the LIGHTS would play (only while Play Along trusts the clock, phase confidence ≥ 0.5).

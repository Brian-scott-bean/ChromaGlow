ChromaGlow
Smart Lighting Controller for Philips Hue — iOS

Privacy Policy

ChromaGlow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device and your local network.

What we access

Microphone. Used locally and in real time to detect sound levels for music-reactive lighting modes. Audio is processed on-device and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Camera. Used only while you scan a QR code someone shares with you (a scene or a home invite). The camera feed is processed on-device and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Location. Used one time, with your permission, to calculate sunrise and sunset for All Day Scenes. Your location is not tracked continuously, stored remotely, or shared.

Local network. ChromaGlow communicates directly with your Philips Hue Bridge over your local Wi-Fi network. No data is routed through any server we control.

What we store

Your Hue Bridge IP address and API token are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. This is the only persistent data ChromaGlow saves. It never leaves your device.

What we don't do

We do not have user accounts. We do not use analytics or crash reporting services. We do not use advertising SDKs. We do not send anything to a cloud server. We do not share any data with third parties.

Third-party services

ChromaGlow communicates only with the Philips Hue Bridge on your local network using the official Hue CLIP v2 API. The only remote service the app may contact is Signify's bridge-discovery endpoint (discovery.meethue.com), used solely to find your bridge's local address when mDNS discovery fails; no personal data is sent.

ChromaGlow is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of Signify (Philips Hue). Philips Hue is a trademark of Signify Holding.

Children

ChromaGlow does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
